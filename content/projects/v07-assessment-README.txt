DIAGNOSTIC DEFINITIONS
Current assets=cash+AR+inventory; current liabilities=AP+current tax payable+
current term debt+revolver. DTL is not current here. Quick numerator=cash+AR.
Debt means term+revolver, not AP. EBIT/interest is coverage; CFO/NI is cash
conversion, with a positive NI denominator. NOPAT=EBIT*(1-25%). IC=AR+
inventory+PPE-AP-current tax payable-DTL=debt+equity-cash. These tax liabilities
are classified as operating for this comparison; this is an explicit extension
of c09's capital bridge, not a universal ROIC definition. Tax payable is not
permanent costless finance. Exclude all cash consistently; do not infer zero
cash need. ROIC/ROE/asset turnover use mean opening/closing IC/equity/assets.
DSO=365*mean AR/annual credit sales. DuPont uses matching asset/equity averages.
Final-date capex and share issues make endpoint averages rough exposure proxies;
their return differences must not be treated as marginal project returns or
the new shareholder's investment return. Show components and denominators.
