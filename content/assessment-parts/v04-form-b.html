Form B — Audit a different mechanism

100 points; 180 minutes; closed notes and closed solutions; basic nonprogrammable calculator. Each of the five essential domains is worth 20 points, with four parts worth 5 points each. Show the calculation and state its target, units and assumptions. Exact fractions and expressions in logarithms or square roots are acceptable; no computer execution or external source retrieval is needed. Use this form after remediation without rehearsing its answers. All records, probability laws and release scenarios below are original synthetic constructions, not empirical observations.

Form B complete solutions and point allocation

Each part totals 5 points. Award method credit when a subsequent arithmetic error is carried consistently; do not award interpretation credit for an incorrect target or unit.   2. D2.

    (a) Python may remove assert statements under optimization, leaving no guard. Moreover, isinstance(True,int) is true, so the original guard accepts a boolean even without optimization. One complete persistent replacement is:

    def validate_margins(xs):
    if type(xs) is not list or not 1 <= len(xs) <= 8:
        raise ValueError("require a list of 1 through 8 margins")
    for x in xs:
        if type(x) is not int or not 0 <= x <= 1_000_000_000:
            raise ValueError("require integer cents in range, excluding bool")
    return xs

    The short-circuit first condition avoids asking for the length of a non-list; exact type checking excludes bool and floats, including nonfinite floats. Scoring: 1 removable-assert failure; 1 bool failure; 1 list/length condition; 1 scalar/range condition; 1 explicit persistent exception.

    (b) Divide the supplied identity by n² to obtain V = Σi<j(xi − xj)²/n², since V = Σxi²/n − x̄². The mean is 100000005 cents. Pairwise squared differences are 9,81,36 cents²; their sum is 126, so V = 126/9 = 14 cents². Centering at the exact mean gives deviations (−4,−1,5) cents, and (16+1+25)/3 = 14 cents² independently. Scoring: 1 identity-to-variance derivation; 1 mean; 1 pairwise sum; 1 variance/units; 1 centered check.

    (c) Dividing each amount by 100 divides squared deviations by 100²; V is 14/10000 = 7/5000 = 0.0014 USD². The n(n−1) variant returns 126/6 = 21 cents², the sample-variance quantity rather than this finite-list population target. The faulty unit conversion returns 14/100 = 0.14 USD², one hundred times too large. In exact arithmetic V(x+k) = V(x), while V(ax) = a²V(x); checking either with changed inputs helps detect errors independently of a single expected output. Scoring: 2 correct squared-unit conversion; 1 each wrong result; 1 valid invariant.

    (d) Write the unrounded vector as x+δ, with each |δi| ≤ η. Its centered perturbation has population variance V(δ) = mean(δ²)−mean(δ)² ≤ η². Expanding centered squares gives ΔV = 2 mean[(x−x̄)(δ−δ̄)] + V(δ). Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the absolute cross term by 2√V√V(δ), giving the stated bound. Here it is √14 + 1/4 ≈ 3.991657 cents², or that value divided by 10000 in USD². The guarantee is conditional on the input-error bound and target formula, not a probability statement about repeated samples. Exact arithmetic and a perturbation bound do not verify margin definitions, coverage or economic representativeness. Scoring: 1 perturbation variance bound; 1 cross-term justification; 1 value/units; 1 no sampling-confidence claim; 1 economic-contract limit.

  4. D4.

    (a) The estimate is p̂=0 and the observed indicators have sample variance zero, giving plug-in MCSE zero. For iid indicators, Pp(K=0)=(1−p)500. Setting this probability equal to 0.05 gives pU=1−0.051/500≈0.005973552=0.5973552%. This is the zero-count endpoint of the exact one-sided 95% binomial confidence procedure. A constant observed sample can miss a rare event; its zero sample variance does not establish zero simulator variance. It is not a 95% posterior probability that p lies below this value. Scoring: 1 estimate/plug-in error; 1 zero-count probability; 1 inversion; 1 numerical bound; 1 correct interpretation.

    (b) Require 1−0.051/n≤0.002, equivalently 0.051/n≥0.998. Taking logs gives log(0.05)/n≥log(0.998), hence n≥log(0.05)/log(0.998)≈1496.367771, reversing the inequality when dividing by the negative denominator. The smallest integer is 1497. This is a planning statement conditional on observing zero; a future positive count requires another bound and might not meet the target. Scoring: 1 inequality with correct sign; 1 minimum integer; 1 zero-count contingency; 1 replication unit; 1 model-error limit.

    (c) Retained baseline states are (A,B)=(0,0),(1,1), each with conditional probability 1/2. The untreated mean is 20 USD and treated mean 28 USD, so the observed gap is 8 USD.
