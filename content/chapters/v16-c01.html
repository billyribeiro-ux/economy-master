Choose a question you can answer and a field you can grow into

Capability. Select a tractable specialization and write an economically significant research question with a defensible first deliverable. Prerequisites are the accepted work on advanced econometrics, economic theory, macro-finance and fields and institutions. This is research design instruction, not a report of new empirical discoveries. The researchers, banks, schedules and records in both worked cases are fictional.

“I want to study financial crises” expresses an interest. It leaves unanswered whose behavior matters, what is observed, which counterfactual is credible, and what a first paper could establish. Conversely, “I will run a fixed-effects regression” selects a tool before a question. A useful agenda connects an important economic uncertainty to a sequence of answerable questions. Its first paper can be small without its motivating question being trivial.

A field is a community organized around recurring questions, institutions and methods. A specialization is the narrower part in which you can evaluate the relevant arguments in detail. An agenda is a connected sequence of questions; a project is one bounded attempt to answer one of them. An estimand is the exact quantity sought. A contribution is the justified change in what a reader can know, explain or do relative to specified existing work. A dataset, estimator or topic by itself is not yet a contribution.

1. Set field boundaries by the economic question

The library's default primary field is macro-finance, with financial econometrics as a supporting field. In this route, macro-finance asks how financial arrangements and aggregate outcomes interact: for example, whether intermediaries' financing constraints change investment after a common disturbance. Financial econometrics supplies measurement and inference tools suited to financial data. The distinction is functional. An estimator can support several fields; using a bank dataset does not automatically make every question macro-finance. A paper about local branch competition may primarily address industrial organization; a paper about loan contract design may address corporate finance; a paper about transmission to aggregate investment has a macro-finance target. These boundaries overlap.

Choose a primary audience by asking whose unresolved argument your result would change. Then name a supporting audience that can expose a weakness: an econometrician for identification, an institutional specialist for measurement, or a theorist for equilibrium consistency. Do not claim that one result settles every audience's concern. If individual banks cut lending while borrowers switch lenders, the aggregate effect may differ. An agenda can investigate that bridge later; an initial bank-level association cannot cross it by assertion.

These choices are the library's design, not formal degree labels. As inspected on 16 October 2026, Harvard Economics' published requirements combine core preparation, fields, research and faculty feedback. HBS Business Economics also describes practitioner engagement as a research activity. Wharton's finance course descriptions connect financial institutions, empirical methods and macro-finance. These are curricular benchmarks and further-study routes, not evidence that this chapter's invented project is novel or institutionally approved.

2. Find an advantage that supports a question

Research comparative advantage is an opportunity-cost comparison, not a claim that you are already the best person in a field. Useful assets include knowledge of accounting definitions, a language, experience reconstructing historical records, a credible collaboration, mathematical skill and lawful data access. Some advantages are temporary; some can be acquired. Access is only valuable if the records measure something relevant and their permitted use supports the intended output.

Separate demonstrated ability from aspiration. “I reproduced a published table from its supplied files and explained its missing observations” is evidence of a skill; “I intend to learn machine learning” is a training plan. Also separate possession from permission: receiving a file does not establish authority to redistribute it. Inspect the actual access terms before designing a deliverable around them. The chapter supplies no general legal rule for any dataset.

Compare candidate projects on a small evidence sheet: the decision or explanation at stake; the nearest existing claim; the data or model needed; access and measurement; the inference bridge; expected bottleneck; and the smallest honest deliverable. Use hard feasibility gates before preferences. If a question requires unavailable borrower identities, its importance cannot compensate for their absence. Among feasible projects, compare fit and learning value in words tied to evidence. A weighted score can organize discussion, but invented scores are not estimates of publication probability.

For theory, the access gate becomes a tractability gate: state a baseline model, its solution concept and a result you can actually establish. Adding financial frictions to an existing model is insufficient if the new assumptions neither change an implication nor clarify its scope. For empirical work, a feasible descriptive paper can be more valuable than a causal claim resting on an unexamined assignment mechanism. Reframing must change the estimand and claim, not just remove the word “causal” from the title.

3. Write a question contract before collecting everything

A question contract names the population, unit, period, outcome, comparison, intended claim and main limitation. For a descriptive mean change, write the population over which the mean is taken and how missing units are handled. For a causal effect, specify treatment and potential outcomes, then explain why observed variation could identify that counterfactual. For a theoretical proposition, specify the domain and assumptions under which it holds. Do not promote a conditional implication into an unconditional empirical explanation.

Use a claim ladder. First, establish what the records contain. Next, describe an association in a specified population. A further step may identify a causal effect under additional assumptions. Moving to a mechanism requires evidence that distinguishes competing channels; moving to a policy conclusion also requires welfare, implementation and transport assumptions. This is a guide to dependencies, not a universal ordering of paper quality: a careful measurement contribution may be the most important output. State the highest rung your evidence supports and the exact missing bridge to the next.

Economic significance asks what uncertainty matters, for whom, and why its resolution could change an explanation or decision. Statistical significance alone cannot answer that question. A precisely estimated tiny average may hide large distributional effects; a wide interval may still rule out a claimed large mechanism. Conversely, a striking estimate with poor measurement can be uninformative. Define the economically relevant scale before examining convenient results, while acknowledging that exploratory learning can legitimately revise the question if the revision is disclosed.

Bound the first paper along several dimensions at once: one primary outcome, a defensible population, a coherent window, one main comparison and a limited claim. Exclusions need reasons. “Only surviving institutions” changes the target and may omit the mechanism of interest. Keep an explicit extension list so excluded questions are remembered without quietly entering the current conclusion. Define a stop or pivot condition that tests feasibility rather than whether the estimate has a desired sign.

Worked case 1. From “crises and credit” to a question and decision map

Mira has reconstructed bank balance sheets in coursework and can explain stock-versus-flow distinctions. She has not worked with confidential loan records. She has twelve weeks at ten research hours per week: (12,10), giving 120 hours. Her broad concern is whether short-term funding makes credit supply fragile. Three possible first projects are on the table. All access statements below are stipulations of this fictional case.

Completed question contract. “Among banks with comparable statements in the fictional system at dates 0 and 1, how does the change in reported loan stock, measured relative to each bank's date-0 loan stock, differ between groups defined by date-0 short-term funding share?” A bank is the unit, the two reporting dates define the window, and only positive baseline loan stocks enter proportional changes. For this contract, short-term funding is funding with remaining contractual maturity at most one year; divide it by positive total funding on the same accounting perimeter. Fix the high-share group at a date-0 share of at least one half and the low-share group below one half, before examining loan changes. Exclude undefined ratios and record their counts. The share is not a measure of an unobserved surprise. The first table will report the matched population, omitted institutions and accounting-definition changes separately. The question concerns loan stocks, not new loan originations, borrowing firms' investment or welfare.

Completed inference map. Funding maturity may expose a bank to refinancing needs; funding composition may also reflect its customers, business model and anticipated risk. Local loan demand and write-offs can change reported loans without a supply shift. The descriptive comparison therefore does not identify the effect of funding maturity. Its immediate economic use is to establish whether an asserted co-movement exists in comparable records and how sensitive that description is to coverage. A causal follow-up needs an assignment argument and evidence separating supply from demand; borrower linkage alone would not solve either problem.

Completed contribution sentence. “The proposed first paper would document the sensitivity of the funding–lending association to a reconciled population and reporting perimeter, and state which claims these records cannot support.” This is a proposed contribution. Its novelty depends on comparing actual nearby papers and data constructions in the next chapter. If existing work already establishes the same result with the same scope and better evidence, the current project becomes a replication or training exercise unless a consequential discrepancy or extension is justified.

Completed feasibility contract. Allocate (20,30,20,30,20) hours to measurement, analysis, literature mapping, writing and reserve respectively; the total is 120. These are planning allowances, not observed productivity. By the end of the measurement allowance, require a documented common definition and an auditable identifier map. If neither exists, stop the association analysis and write a measurement discrepancy memo. If the map works, retain excluded units and reasons in an attrition table. A zero or reversed association is a reportable result under the same design. Changing the window until the desired sign appears is not the pivot rule.

Agenda map. The first project establishes a measurement and descriptive baseline. A second project would examine a specific funding disturbance only if assignment and a comparison are defensible. A third could connect credit reallocation to firm investment and aggregate outcomes if borrower coverage and an aggregation model become available. These are conditional branches, not three promised publishable papers. Mira schedules feedback from a banking specialist about definitions and an econometrician about the intended counterfactual. This makes the macro-finance ambition concrete while keeping the first claim bounded.

4. Preserve the agenda when a project changes

A research agenda should survive learning that a favorite design is impossible. Maintain a decision log containing the original question, new information, resulting change in claim and remaining value. A pivot can be good research judgment if it follows an observed constraint. It is weaker if it merely hides a disappointing estimate. Record both; exploratory results can motivate later confirmatory work, but they cannot retroactively become a prespecified test.

Distinguish a new setting from a new inference. A setting can matter if it changes the mechanism, population, institution or information available, and the paper demonstrates why. “No one has studied this country with this estimator” is a search impression, not an argument that the answer changes knowledge. Likewise, a negative finding is informative when measurement and precision can discriminate among substantive claims. “The coefficient was insignificant” does not establish an effect of zero.

The following invented loan-stock records are measured in millions of the same currency. “Missing” means not observed in that file, not a zero stock, a new bank or a closed bank. The machine-readable order (A0,A1,B0,B1,C1,D0) is (40,36,60,54,90,20). The raw file totals are (120,180), an observed-file increase of 1/2, or 50%. For the matched banks the totals are (100,90), a change of -1/10, or −10%. A and B each decline by 10%, so the equally weighted mean of their proportional changes is also -1/10. This equality is special to these inputs; it is not a general identity between a mean growth rate and growth of a sum.

The exact observed-total decomposition is (matched change,newly observed stock,previously observed stock,total change)=(-10,90,20,60): −10+90−20=60. The data establish that differing coverage can reverse the sign of the descriptive headline. They do not establish that system-wide lending increased, that C was created, that D closed or that a funding shock caused the matched decline. The missing values prevent calculation of the full four-bank change.

Completed abstract for this demonstration. “A four-bank synthetic example contrasts changes in raw-file totals with changes for matched reporting units. Raw totals rise by 50% while matched totals fall by 10%. The difference is explained exactly by coverage components. The example motivates an audit of reporting populations before a funding–lending association is interpreted. It supplies no estimate for a real banking system and no causal effect.” The title is “Coverage and the interpretation of bank loan-stock changes: a synthetic design exercise.” Calling it “Funding shocks reduce national investment” would exceed both the outcome and the identification evidence.

Completed revision log. Original ambition: estimate a funding-shock effect on firm investment. New information: linkage unavailable, assignment unresolved, and a synthetic audit illustrates coverage sensitivity. Revised first deliverable: a documented descriptive design, followed by a real-data measurement audit only when lawful usable records are obtained. Retained economic motivation: evaluating the premise that banks' funding and lending move together. Deferred claims: supply causation, firm investment and general equilibrium. Next decisive test: compare actual reporting definitions and identifiers, not collect more regressions on mismatched totals.

Completed boundary witness. Fill the two missing entries only as hypothetical possibilities. If (C0,D1)=(90,20), full totals are (210,200,-1/21), where the final entry is proportional growth. If (C0,D1)=(0,100), full totals are (120,280,4/3). Both completions preserve every observed cell and keep loan stocks nonnegative, yet full-system growth has opposite signs. Therefore the observed records alone do not identify even the sign of that growth. Neither completion is an imputation endorsed for real data. The useful next step is obtaining evidence about missingness, not choosing the completion that favors a preferred story.

5. Exercises: turn an interest into a defensible project

Use the supplied facts for determinate answers. For open-ended tasks, alternatives earn full credit when they satisfy every stated criterion and explicitly bound the claim. The illustrative answers are complete examples, not mandatory topics. No exercise establishes actual novelty or your completed research competence.

  1. Ada needs 8 hours for a specified record-reconstruction task and 20 for a specified model task. Bo needs 12 and 24 hours respectively. The ordered hours (Ada reconstruction, Ada model, Bo reconstruction, Bo model) are (8,20,12,24). Assume divisible tasks, constant rates and equal output quality. Who has absolute advantage in each task? Compare each person's reconstruction cost in model-task units and identify comparative advantage. Explain why this alone does not select a dissertation field.

Complete solutions and evaluation criteria

1. Advantage depends on the alternative forgone

Ada has absolute advantage in both tasks because each takes fewer hours. The reconstruction opportunity costs are (Ada,Bo)=(2/5,1/2) model tasks; Ada has comparative advantage in reconstruction. Model-task opportunity costs are (Ada,Bo)=(5/2,2) reconstruction tasks, so Bo has comparative advantage in modeling. These are two tasks under fixed rates, not comprehensive research production functions. Interest, the importance and feasibility of a question, complementary skills, future learning and collaborator availability remain relevant. The comparison does not prove a field ranking or an optimal staffing assignment with unspecified deadlines and indivisibilities.

2. Audience follows the unresolved argument

(a) Industrial organization or banking competition is a defensible primary audience; an institutional banking specialist can test pricing definitions. (b) Macro-finance is primary; financial econometrics can assess inference and a banking specialist can assess mechanisms. (c) Econometrics is primary; an empirical-finance audience can assess whether the dependence model matches the application. Credit equivalent defensible boundaries with reasons. Data ownership does not determine field: the three projects ask about market structure, aggregate transmission and inferential validity respectively. Calling (c) macro-finance solely because its panel contains banks omits the methodological target.

3. Fix the population and weighting

For matched set M={A,B}, the estimand is [Σᵢ∈M Lᵢ1−Σᵢ∈M Lᵢ0]/Σᵢ∈M Lᵢ0, requiring positive total baseline loans. Initially it equals −1/10. With A1=44, matched loans become 98 and growth is -1/50, or −2%. Individual growth rates are 1/10 and −1/10, whose equal-weight mean is 0. Aggregate growth weights these rates by baseline loan shares 2/5 and 3/5, so the larger declining bank receives greater weight. Neither C nor D enters these matched estimands. Their omission remains crucial to any claim about a wider population; equal and loan-weighted quantities answer different questions.

4. Rewrite the question and the promise

“For banks with comparable statements at dates 0 and 1, how does proportional reported loan-stock change differ between groups defined before the window by short-term funding share?” Unit: bank; population: matched reporters with positive initial loans; outcome: stock growth; comparison: baseline-defined funding groups; claim: descriptive difference in this population. No policy effect is identified. A decisive missing bridge is a defensible assignment/comparison argument separating policy exposure from other determinants of lending; firm investment measurement would be an additional bridge. A complete answer must specify all these components and mention coverage and loan-stock interpretation. Replacing “prove” with “estimate” without changing the unsupported target is insufficient.

5. Use the reserve without inventing time

The revised allocation is (35,30,20,30,5), totaling 120 hours; reserve falls to five. With 25 extra hours, the original substantive allowances plus extra work total 125, exceeding capacity by five. Seek an explicit scope reduction, revise the deadline or stop at a measurement memo. Do not claim the original design fits or remove required validation silently. A defensible scope reduction might postpone group comparisons while preserving definition reconciliation and a clearly labeled coverage audit; it changes the deliverable and should be recorded.

6. Turn novelty into a comparison that can be checked

The sentence asserts an unverified exhaustive search and does not explain why either geography or estimator changes knowledge. A replacement is: “This proposed paper will test whether a descriptive funding–lending pattern persists when reporting definitions and the observed bank population are reconciled. Its intended value is to show which part of an apparent aggregate change reflects within-bank change and which reflects file coverage. The claim is descriptive and conditional on usable comparable records; novelty remains to be established.” Strengthening evidence would identify specific closest papers, their populations and treatment of changing coverage, and explain a consequential unresolved difference. If those papers already perform an equivalent audit on the same scope with better evidence, retain this as replication/training or justify a distinct discrepancy. A new estimator label alone does not rescue the contribution. Grade the comparison, significance, feasibility and honest novelty boundary, not rhetorical excitement.

7. Let the evidence change the claim

Log: “Original target: the prespecified descriptive association. New information: the interval is too wide to discriminate small effects from substantively different values, and a justified measurement correction changes the sign. Action: retain the correction as the main definition, report the original as a documented sensitivity comparison, explain the perimeter difference, and disclose any exploratory windows. Claim: no stable directional association established under the examined definitions; causal effects remain unidentified. Deliverable: an auditable measurement note and a design for obtaining consistent coverage.” The wide interval is absence of decisive evidence, not evidence of a zero effect. Evidence of absence would require an estimand and uncertainty narrow enough to exclude effects judged important under credible assumptions. A sign change caused by measurement is a separate uncertainty; a preferred p-value does not resolve it. Full credit requires disclosure, a justified definition and a next action based on the weakness, rather than suppression of unfavorable results.

8. Preserve the method while changing the field

One complete contract: “Within a fixed set of neighborhoods and a specified collection window, how does the distribution of advertised monthly rent for a defined dwelling type differ across neighborhoods, after documenting duplicate advertisements and observed dwelling attributes?” Housing/urban economics is primary; measurement and econometrics support comparability. Proposed contribution: a transparent description of advertised-market composition and how deduplication changes the comparison, conditional on a literature review showing its relevance. Artifact: a permitted, documented sample, variable dictionary, deduplication log and descriptive table with coverage limitations; restricted raw listings need not be redistributed. Stop or pivot to a measurement memo if terms do not permit the intended analysis or duplicates cannot be evaluated. A next project could compare advertisements with transactions if lawful linkage and quality measures become available. Advertised rents are asking prices for selected listings; they omit many incumbent residents, accepted transaction prices, housing quality changes, incomes, displacement and benefits. Hence they do not identify resident welfare. Grade all requested contract components and the specific inference boundary; equivalent well-bounded designs are acceptable.

6. Teach-back and delayed transfer

Teach-back. In three minutes, explain why a feasible descriptive first paper can serve a causal macro-finance agenda. Use case 2 to name one exact inference it permits and two it does not. Then say what new evidence would justify advancing one claim.

Teach-back answer guide

A sound account distinguishes the agenda from the first paper. Case 2 demonstrates a coverage decomposition and a sign reversal between two observed-population summaries. It cannot establish full-system growth or a funding-shock effect. Recovering the missing comparable stocks would permit the full four-bank growth calculation; it would still not identify causation. A causal claim needs a separately defended counterfactual design. Do not equate a clean accounting identity with identification.

Delayed retention. After a week, without copying the templates, write a six-sentence question contract for a new field. Then an adviser reports that your key measurement is unavailable. Revise the claim and deliverable while preserving an economically meaningful question. Supply one condition under which you should abandon the project.

Retention criteria and illustrative answer

Require a defined population/unit, period, outcome, comparison, supported claim and limitation; then a change tied to the unavailable measurement, not a preferred result. Example: “I study advertised vacancies for a defined job category across two specified cities during one collection window. A vacancy advertisement is the unit. The outcome is its posted wage, with missing wages reported separately. I compare distributions after deduplicating advertisements. The target is a description of posted wages, not accepted wages or worker welfare. Differences may reflect selected employers and jobs.” If hours are unavailable, do not silently compare monthly and hourly offers: restrict to a documented common pay unit or write a measurement audit. Abandon the comparison if neither comparable units nor a meaningful narrower population can be established. The illustrative answer is a research design, not a finding about actual labor markets.
