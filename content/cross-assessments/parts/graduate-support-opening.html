Graduate qualifying sequence: administration and evidence

This sequence follows the accepted econometrics, graduate microeconomic theory and macro-finance volumes. It evaluates transfer across their methods: a numerical answer must belong to a valid model, an identification argument must correspond to the observed data, and a policy claim must identify preferences, resources and private incentives. It is an original library assessment. Passing it is not a university qualification, an institutional endorsement, or evidence of completed original research.

There are three separate field examinations, each with an initial form and a substantively different reassessment form. Each form has five20-point domains, five20-point problems and four5-point subparts per problem. Total100per field form. Allow300minutes per form as an uncalibrated design allowance, on separate days. Follow the field's stated resource rules; if a field does not specify an exception, use a basic calculator with closed notes for derivations, and open the library and standard-library Python only for explicitly computational or research-audit subparts. Preserve code and intermediate reasoning where computation is allowed. The supplied complete solutions are instructor references; reproducing them after reading is practice, not an independent attempt.

Advancement requires at least85overall and15in every20-point field domain on an independent form, correction of all critical errors, and a changed-primitive oral defense. These criterion thresholds and the relative difficulty of forms have not been empirically calibrated. Never combine scores across fields to conceal a missing core skill. A funding, probability, identification, terminal-condition, integrity or feasible-deviation error requires remediation even with a high total. An evaluator may award method credit for a correctly propagated arithmetic slip; silently replacing the problem's maintained assumptions does not count as a valid solution.

Use FormA first and reserve FormB for reassessment after correction and a delay. Record which materials and solutions were seen, elapsed time and accommodation, assistance used, every missed domain and the substantive error. Accommodations can alter time or presentation while preserving the skill being assessed; do not interpret uncalibrated speed differences as differences in research ability. Research proposals admit defensible alternative mechanisms and conclusions; grade their logic, feasible evidence and clear limits, not agreement with a particular policy.

A successful attempt supports the tested derivations and explanations under their explicit domains. Independent expert assessment remains necessary for a research contribution, a field specialization and scholarly judgment.
