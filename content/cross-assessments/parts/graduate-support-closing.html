Remediation and reassessment record

For every missed question retain the original work, quote the failed equation or inference, state its missing premise and solve the corrected problem. Then solve a changed-primitive version without consulting the answer. A correction copied from the model solution records exposure to the method, not mastery. After a study interval, attempt the other field form under recorded conditions and repeat the oral task with an evaluator-chosen, independently solved variant. Do not average the first and second scores; retain both and explain the change.

Evaluator protocol and honest record

Record the field/form, date, resources and accommodations, problem/domain scores, critical errors, oral transcript or notes, corrections and reassessment. A human subject expert should judge advanced proof gaps, empirical design and research interpretation; the included solutions and numerical checks assist that judgment but cannot replace it. If no evaluator is available, label the result self-study completion with unevaluated oral work. Do not issue an invented external credential or claim that the learner has completed research, teaching or leadership experience.

Disagreements deserve a recorded reason: compare the exact maintained assumptions and target claim, allow alternative valid proofs, and distinguish an arithmetic slip from a model change. If a benchmark has an error, preserve the original question, document the correction and regrade affected work consistently. The library's production reviews check these supplied problems; they are not observations of a learner's performance.
