Investment-policy project — Tern Scholarship Pool

Capability. Turn a small, imperfect research database into a transparent prospective investment decision, fund the decision on its payment dates, implement an authorized trading plan and report the outcome without rewriting its history. This original open-resource project is designed for 14–18 hours, an author planning estimate. It integrates dated returns, covariance, constrained allocation, factor interpretation, search controls, manager evaluation, nontradable commitments, private-asset liquidity, execution and policy governance. Complete an independent first submission before opening the reference output or instructor guide.

Tern funds scholarships while holding an existing private investment. Its research team proposes a manager with an attractive short record; its board also faces a capital call that can grow during bad markets. The trading desk may be unable to complete the intended bond sale. Your job is to connect these facts: identify which research was actually available, decide how much weight it deserves, protect dated cash and preserve the benchmark against which the decision will be judged. A manager can win a research screen yet receive no capital under the board's chosen uncertainty rule.

A disclosed planning model

Use the four available training E/B returns to compute sample covariance with denominator n − 1 = 3. The committee chooses a 50:50 blend of four times that matrix with the annual prior [[0.04, 0.004], [0.004, 0.01]]. Four times quarterly covariance is an additive annual planning proxy, requiring an equal-period independent-shock interpretation; it is not the exact variance of compounded annual returns. The prior and the 50% intensity are supplied policy choices. Covariance shrinkage supplies the method of combining a sample and structured target, not evidence that this fixed blend is optimal.

The board separately supplies annual E/B mean returns of 8% and 3%. Let their zero-mean deviations be FE and FB with blended covariance Σ. The chosen manager's prospective net return is 0.4(0.08 + FE) + 0.6(0.03 + FB) + a − 0.008 + εM. Its annual fee is already subtracted. This prospective annual fee convention is distinct from the quarterly fees in the research records. The uncertain gross overlay a belongs to [0, 0.012], without a probability distribution, and Var(εM) = 0.0016. Neither the overlay nor the forward residual variance is estimated from the four-point sample fit.

The private whole-claim terminal payoff is 320 + 140FE + 40FB + εP, with Var(εP) = 400 CU². Both residuals are orthogonal to both factors and to each other. The private factor coefficients are CU; manager coefficients are return loadings. Planning calls and grants are fixed at call 80/grant 60 at date 1, grant 40 at date 2. This moment model treats those payments as deterministic. Different calls in the stress register are separate conditional scenarios, not a silently added source of probabilistic variance.

Derive the full covariance of the two index returns, manager return and private payout, then the total and active variances for a candidate. Supply a bounded joint law realizing these moments with positive baseline gross payoffs. A self-contained scaffold uses independent equiprobable signs U1, U2 and sets FE = AU1, FB = BU1 + CU2. Match A² = ΣEE, AB = ΣEB and B² + C² = ΣBB, choosing A and C positive. Two further independent signs drive the manager and private residuals. This construction demonstrates consistency, not calibration, no-arbitrage pricing or an empirical return distribution. The distinction between physical moments and pricing restrictions remains essential.

For each feasible portfolio, maximize the worst-over-a value of expected terminal reserve minus γ times total variance divided by twice initial wealth. Also compute the nominal optimum at a = 0.012 over the same feasible set. Break ties by lower total variance, then lower entry cost, then lexicographic E/B/manager holdings. Explain why the worst mean occurs at the lower overlay endpoint here, and why that need not follow if short manager exposure were permitted.

  3. Risk derivation and a coherent joint law

    The E/B sample means are 0.01 and 0.005. Their centered cross products sum to 0.0052, −0.0014 and 0.0005. Dividing by three gives SEE = 13/7500, SEB = −7/15000 and SBB = 1/6000. The chosen blend is Σ = [[44/1875, 2/1875], [2/1875, 2/375]]. Its leading entry is positive and determinant is 436/3515625 > 0, so it is positive definite. The sign of its cross term differs from the small sample's because the prior contributes positive cross-covariance. No ranking success proves that the prior or intensity was well chosen.

    Write the random claim vector as H = (rE, rB, rM, DP). With loadings L = [[1,0], [0,1], [0.4,0.6], [140,40]], its covariance is LΣL′ + diag(0,0,0.0016,400). For every vector w, w′Cov(H)w = (L′w)′Σ(L′w) + 0.0016wM² + 400wP² ≥ 0. The private row/column has mixed CU-return units; the final quadratic form with positions (x,y,z,1) has units CU². This construction exposes both manager/private cross-covariances through common factors. Assuming all alternative assets are independent would discard those terms.

    For a concrete finite witness, take independent signs U1, U2, UM, UP, each ±1 with probability 1/2. Define A = √ΣEE, B = ΣEB/A and C = √(ΣBB − ΣEB²/ΣEE). Put FE = AU1, FB = BU1 + CU2, εM = 0.04UM, εP = 20UP. The 16 equally weighted states reproduce all means, variances and orthogonalities. Approximately A = 0.153188337, B = 0.006963106, C = 0.072696963. The manager deviation is bounded by 0.149071377; the private deviation by 44.63277 CU. Even the lower-overlay manager gross payoff and private payout are positive in every state. These are stipulated physical moments, not a calibrated likelihood or set of state prices; the stress register has its own conditional scope.

    More generally, for target holdings t and actual h at initial price one, let Δ = t − h, terminal factors G and fees Kt, Kh. Since ct − ch = −ΣΔ + Kh − Kt, target minus actual terminal wealth is ΣΔ(G − 1) + (Kh − Kt)g² + (ΣΔ)(1 − g²). The last term is zero here because the two missing notionals offset. This derivation preserves cash's foregone accrual and every unfilled target leg; it does not assume a displayed quote guarantees execution.

    A terminal reserve is not a total-pool return. The private call transfers money within the pool, whereas scholarships are external flows. Independent private values at external-flow boundaries are absent, so a total-pool TWR cannot be recovered by pretending the private carrying value remained constant. The matched portfolios nevertheless have the same dated external obligations and private rights, making their terminal-wealth difference informative. It measures a realized policy comparison, not proof of managerial skill, an equilibrium alpha or a causal performance effect.

  7. An acceptable IPS and decision memo

    A defensible benchmark recommendation adopts the robust target (325,250,0), retains K as the screened manager for further diligence, authorizes the specified staged-order fallback and keeps the strategic benchmark fixed. The board deliberately trades a small upper-overlay score advantage for a better minimum score under its interval. The memo should acknowledge the thin risk margin of the nominal alternative and the robust policy's small design-stress reserve headroom. The outside-design stress is a genuine residual vulnerability; a positive ordinary expected reserve is not a promise to meet every possible grant.

    The IPS should identify scholarship beneficiaries, the existing private obligation, a one-year decision horizon and its half-year cash bottleneck. Assign the board responsibility for objectives, ambiguity interval, risk limits, benchmark and any donor support; the investment staff for the vintage/evidence register and independent model; the treasury function for payment-date cash; the desk for bond-sale-first execution within the authorized continuation rule; and an independent reviewer for recomputing results and reporting exceptions. These are proposed case governance roles, not legal obligations. The dated institutional IPS reference supports connecting objectives, responsibilities, liquidity, benchmarks and monitoring; the numerical limits and recommendation are original choices.

    Before releasing the equity order, treasury verifies actual settled sale proceeds and recomputes every remaining constraint. At each payment date it confirms the private call notice, grant amount, liquid buffer and sale authority. Risk monitoring uses current exposures and reports both total and active risk, with a record of whether covariance changes are new planning assumptions or genuine new evidence. Performance reporting retains the January target, the authorized actual holdings, the fixed benchmark, the period/vintage labels, fees and unfilled orders. A changed limit, manager forecast, private sale right, donor commitment or new contingency requires a dated decision; the report preserves the old policy for attribution.

    At least five concrete evidence requests should connect uncertainty to action. Examples: obtain timestamped manager records and revision controls to assess the selection history; reconcile fee bases and net performance to cash records before comparing managers; obtain a longer unselected sample and a truly prospective research protocol before increasing the overlay; inspect private capital-call rights and notice periods before approving the buffer; obtain executable bond liquidity/fee terms and settlement timing before increasing trade size; verify private distributions and valuation independence before presenting a whole-pool return; examine how risk choices change across defensible priors and residual covariances before adopting a higher risk budget. Generic requests to “do more research” earn little credit.

    Full judgment credit does not require professing certainty in γ = 4 or a 700-CU reserve floor. A learner may recommend a more conservative policy, a smaller private commitment at a future contractual opportunity, or negotiating additional support. They must first reproduce the assigned optimum, price the recommendation's opportunity costs under stated assumptions, identify the authority and funds it requires, and show what cannot currently be changed. They must not claim the existing private stake is readily liquid merely because a recommendation needs cash.

    For a five-minute defense, require the learner to trace one observation from release to decision, one covariance term from common factor to total wealth, and one sale from original units to reduced future proceeds. An instructor may withhold the reference output until the independent submission is fixed. Evidence of reasoning, honest uncertainty and reconciled cash earns credit; a polished presentation does not compensate for an unavailable input or an unfunded commitment.

What the completed project establishes

The deliverable establishes that the learner can audit a dated synthetic record, implement a transparent bounded policy, conserve cash and claims, and defend the limits of an inference. It does not validate actual manager skill, the board's risk preferences, a traded private valuation or compliance with a performance standard. The next extension is a prospectively specified study with real accessible data, complete inclusion rules, appropriate uncertainty analysis and independent review. Preserve these same information, funding and reporting disciplines when the data become larger.
