Methods, scope and remediation

Problems use the accepted chapter methods linked in the coverage map. Their numerical configurations and diagnostic demands are original. The source record reuses the exact earlier chapter inspection scopes; this assessment adds no newly retrieved primary body, empirical calibration or technical theorem attributed to an unread source. Read the full chapter proof when a solution cites a maintained assumption rather than treating a five-point answer as a survey of the entire field.

For remediation, record the failed equation and its domain, solve the corrected model, then change one primitive or constraint and reconstruct the result. Reattempt a different configuration after a delay and explain whose resources pay for each claim. A high total does not erase an unresolved funding, admissibility, expectation, identification or equilibrium-selection error.
