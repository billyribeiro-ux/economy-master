3. Global model, exact inference and identification limits

Global optimum proof. J is continuous on compact[0,K], so a maximizer exists. For h>0, J″=−h<0, so it is unique whenever the interval contains more than one point; when K=0the only feasible choice is0. Let q=(a−r)/h. Completing the square gives J(x)=−h(x−q)²/2+(a−r)²/(2h). Maximizing J therefore minimizes distance to qover[0,K], yielding x*=max(0,min(K,q)). If a−r≤0, choose0; if0<a−r<hK, choose(a−r)/h; if a−r≥hK, chooseK. Equality at either boundary gives the same unique value. This proves global optimality, including corners, rather than merely reporting a first-order condition.

With a=r₀, subsidy s produces xᵢ(s)=min(Kᵢ,s/hᵢ). Capacity starts binding at hK=(5,14/3,9/2). Below its threshold a type's derivative is1/h; above it the derivative is0, with a kink at the threshold. Borrower gain is(1−λ)x, so it is weakly increasing in s only if λ≤1is maintained. With λ>1it can fall as the supported bank expands. The subsidy spending identity is sΣxᵢ(s), which includes transfers to modeled lending rather than net additional borrower credit. It is a budget account, not a social welfare calculation.

Design expectation. In a pair with cohorts i,j, let Yᵢ(z)be the date1total under own offer status zand let Bᵢbe the fixed baseline. When iis offered the pair statistic is[Yᵢ(1)−Bᵢ]−[Yⱼ(0)−Bⱼ]; when jis offered it is[Yⱼ(1)−Bⱼ]−[Yᵢ(0)−Bᵢ]. Averaging these equally likely cases cancels baseline terms and gives{[Yᵢ(1)−Yᵢ(0)]+[Yⱼ(1)−Yⱼ(0)]}/2. Averaging the three pairs gives the equal-cohort six-unit mean assignment effect. The same argument applies to each component. It assumes outcomes do not depend on other cohorts' assignments; no arithmetic can establish that assumption. The observed estimate need not equal the estimand in this one realization.

Exact sharp-null test. Under Yᵢ(1)=Yᵢ(0)for every cohort, observed changes are fixed. Flipping which cohort is offered flips a pair difference's sign. The eight independent sign patterns are equally likely; use the absolute mean as the prespecified two-sided statistic. Version0reference means are (-6,-14/3,-14/3,-10/3,10/3,14/3,14/3,6). Version1reference means are (-2,-2/3,-2/3,2/3,-2/3,2/3,2/3,2). Both have two values at least as extreme as their observed mean, so(tail,count,p)=(2,8,1/4). Neither rejects at5%. The smallest attainable two-sided p-value for these nonzero symmetric endpoint cases is1/4, so this tiny design cannot deliver5%rejection with this statistic even for an extreme observed sign pattern.

For size, order the eight absolute reference values from greatest to least and assign each value its inclusive upper-tail probability. Ties receive the entire tied upper tail. At most an αfraction of assignments can have a tail probability at mostα; uniform assignment therefore gives Pr(p≤α)≤α. This finite proof preserves the actual paired lottery. It is a sharp-null test: a zero average with offsetting individual effects does not fix all missing counterfactuals, so the sign-flip list is not automatically a weak-average-null distribution. Nonrejection does not establish zero.

A concrete identification distinction. The observed corrected table is compatible with a constant total effect2for every cohort: set every untreated total change to2and every treated total change to4. It is also compatible with zero effects for every cohort, with fixed changes4for A,C,E and2for B,D,F. Both schedules reproduce the displayed assignment. Their likelihoods over repeated assignments differ, and randomization supplies an inferential basis, but the one realization does not reveal each missing potential outcome. Likewise, the fitted λvalues do not uniquely identify a structural funding channel; a different demand schedule or interaction can generate the same observed components. Treat Note B as a conditional mechanism model and test its restrictions in new evidence rather than label a fit proof of mechanism.

Funding support, borrower substitution and a consequential ledger correction

Abstract. This synthetic research note reconstructs an apparent6million borrower-credit contrast from a paired-offer ledger and documents a restatement that reduces it to2million per cohort. Origin-bank lending remains12million above the paired comparison, with an offsetting−10million other-lender contrast. Under a stipulated three-pair lottery and no interference, the exact two-sided sharp-null p-value is1/4. A constrained funding-cost model can reproduce the component pattern, but its parameters are assumed and its national response is not identified. The exercise shows why version control, outcome scope and inferential limits belong in the main claim.

1. Question and contribution. Lending at a supported bank is not the same outcome as total credit available to its original borrowers. A borrower can shift lenders, and an erroneous component balance can change the apparent net expansion. This note's contribution is an explicit instructional reconstruction connecting those two issues. It claims neither new empirical evidence nor a gap established in the banking literature. The bounded related-method map above explains why linked borrower data and lender-side exposure matter; further substantive literature review is required before an original research claim.
