Remediation and chapter coverage

Use the failed claim to choose remediation. Re-read the assigned chapter's full example, explain the original error, solve a materially changed task, then present a teach-back before returning to assessment. A correct copied solution is a study artifact; record independent reassessment separately. The domains in the written forms sample all twelve outcomes but do not test every possible teaching situation.

Volume synthesis and continuation

Disciplinary correctness remains essential when teaching or leading discussion. A clear explanation of the wrong quantity teaches an error. An accurate key without a useful task does not reveal understanding. A useful response without its support conditions can misrepresent independence. A strong portfolio joins correct economic reasoning with coherent design, exact learner evidence, substantive feedback and actual review.

For the limited portfolio-organization principle, this package reuses the bounded official CMU portfolio guidance inspected for Chapter12: selected evidence needs context and explanation. No new whole-source inspection, causal teaching-effect claim or institutional endorsement is made. Every support task and scoring rule here is original. Continue to the actual teaching portfolio with your own artifacts and human observation. Volume18 adds institutional purpose, budgets, incentives and accountability; a leader must evaluate the quality of teaching and research evidence as carefully as financial evidence.
