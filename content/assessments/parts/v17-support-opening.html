Volume 17 assessment — teaching, communication and research supervision

Design an explanation, inspect a learner's reasoning, revise an instructional decision and defend what the evidence actually shows.

This package separates a prerequisite diagnostic, two written forms, a reproducible teaching rehearsal, oral transfer and delayed retention. All supplied data, learner responses, observer reports and institutional cases are original synthetic specimens. Solving them demonstrates performance on the exercise. Actual teaching and supervision require the learner's own work, appropriate evidence handling, human observation and substantive independent review. Keep first attempts, help received, feedback and revisions. A prepared observation form is not an observation.

Administration and evidence rules

Forms A and B each contain twenty five-point prompts in five20-point domains. Proposed duration is240 minutes, closed book with basic calculator and supplied materials, without code or external search. Require85/100 overall and15/20 in every domain, correction of critical errors, and an unseen transfer response. Form B is a distinct reassessment, not a guarantee of psychometric equivalence. A grader should award the stated criterion points for equivalent valid reasoning, record carried-through arithmetic separately from a changed target, and require actual written artifacts where requested.

The five domains are alignment/explanation; course/seminar design; assessment/feedback/learning evidence; communication/supervision; and observation/portfolio judgment. A technically wrong solution, unsupported causal claim, invented observation or misleading statement of actual access must be repaired even when the total is high. Numerical thresholds do not certify teaching, mentorship or institutional readiness.

The project is open resource and includes actual execution of the supplied reproducible evidence audit. Its synthetic teaching record is rehearsal. The separate cross-volume teaching portfolio requires the learner's own undergraduate and graduate designs, materials, assessment, observed teaching and supervision evidence, with actual human evaluation. Proposed plans and blank forms stay labeled as such. For real learner records, follow actual permission/access arrangements and preserve necessary context through authorized channels.
