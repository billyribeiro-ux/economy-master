Fictional Point Note — what an additional restriction buys

A complete synthetic population has treatment share P(D=1)=1/3. For every treated person the observed outcome is6; for every untreated person it is3. Potential outcomes Y(1),Y(0) lie in[0,9]; consistency means Y=DY(1)+(1−D)Y(0). The target is population ATE E[Y(1)−Y(0)]. No assignment mechanism or independence is given. Define u=E[Y(1)|D=0] and v=E[Y(0)|D=1]. The note additionally assumes both potential-outcome means are independent of D. Under that added mean restriction u=6 and v=3; the note concludes ATE=3. The note explicitly presents a conditional identification result, not a test of the restriction or an estimate from real data.
