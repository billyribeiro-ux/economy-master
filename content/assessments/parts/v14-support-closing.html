Complete solutions and additive scoring

  1. Each wealth class is closed; within it the invariant income law is(1/2,1/2). Therefore every joint law (t/2,t/2,(1−t)/2,(1−t)/2),0≤t≤1, is invariant in wealth-major order. Conversely stationarity forces each within-class income mass to split equally, so this is the entire family. Positive income transitions never move wealth and cannot establish joint irreducibility. A continuous Euler equality is not a necessary condition for a discrete-grid action; compare every feasible grid alternative or prove its Bellman inequality. An invariant distribution alone says nothing about that policy’s optimality.

Remediation and reassessment

Keep an error ledger with the exact prompt, mistaken inference, corrected derivation, violated assumption and a new transfer problem. Classify an error by its cause rather than by the chapter title: a sign mistake in a foreign balance sheet differs from failing to name the currency numeraire; a grid residual differs from the original Bellman residual.

  • Budget, terminal or ownership error: redraw every dated real resource and claim flow, label counterparties, and sum the accounts before optimizing. Return to chapters1,2,6,8,10.
  • Optimization or equilibrium error: list every feasible action and boundary, distinguish necessary conditions from a global comparison, and check every market. Return to chapters1–5and8–10.
  • Distribution error: build the joint transition, find closed classes and verify mass, invariance and initial conditions. Return to chapter3and the quantitative project.
  • Pricing or leverage error: state numeraire, payoff date, investor opportunity set and funding. Verify the same payoff under both strategies before alleging arbitrage. Return to chapters7–10.
  • Evidence error: write the observation law and moment map, separate numerical/parameter/model uncertainty, and disclose adaptation of held-out data. Return to chapters11–12and Volume12.

Redo the failed part from blank paper, explain it aloud, then solve a genuinely changed problem before taking the alternate written form. A correct copied solution is a study aid, not evidence of mastery. Record unresolved conceptual errors for a human instructor rather than compensating for them with extra calculation points.

Volume synthesis

Dynamic optimization, equilibrium accounting and empirical identification are complementary. A household policy may be optimal but fail aggregate clearing; a price system may clear a finite market yet say little about a causal policy effect; a calibrated model may reproduce targets while leaving its central mechanism unidentified. Distributional states, nominal and financial constraints, and international claims enrich the mechanisms but do not remove these logical distinctions.

Carry forward three habits: derive incentives over the actual feasible set and every relevant history; consolidate counterparties before reporting aggregate resources or welfare; and state what observable contrast could reject the interpretation. The graduate qualifying sequence combines these macro-finance skills with econometrics and microeconomic theory. Economic fields and institutions then supplies substantive settings; the research, teaching and leadership portfolios require work and evaluation beyond reading a textbook.
