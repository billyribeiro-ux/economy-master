Pricing-and-risk toolkit project — Alder's dated promise

Capability. Build and defend a small pricing system in which contract rights, valuation inputs, numerical methods and actual cash funding agree. This original open-resource graduate project is designed for 18–24 hours, an uncalibrated author planning estimate. Complete all twelve Volume 10 chapters first. Submit an independent implementation and initial decision before opening the reference output or instructor guide. A collection of chapter-lab screenshots does not satisfy the project: the modules must exchange the same dates, discount factors, rights and units.

Alder's desk proposes a coupon promise with an equity put payment. Its salesperson quotes 108.5 currency units (CU). A reserve manager wants to buy a cheaper corporate promise with the same scheduled coupons, while a quant presents a narrow simulation interval as evidence that the whole transaction is ready. You must decide which parts of that argument survive an independent valuation and funding audit. The task ends with a conditional desk decision and a reproducible evidence package; it does not end when a price prints successfully.

Files, provenance and the information flow

Download the complete synthetic inputs, standard-library reference toolkit, full baseline output, four-payload hash manifest and standalone execution and domain guide. Run python3 v10-assessment-analysis.py, or python3 v10-assessment-analysis.py --input copy.json --output result.json. The code also works under python3 -O; its default input is beside the script. No installation or network is needed. Exact financial inputs are strings; exact rational outputs remain strings. Logarithms, normal probabilities, simulation and scenario trading use explicitly approximate floating arithmetic.

Every number, institution, path, quote, payment rule and decision threshold was invented for this case. None is an observed market value or evidence of real authority. Preserve the baseline and give each intervention a new input/output pair. The information flow is dated instrument cash and quotes → curve → liability reserve and rate exposures; the curve's first segment also supplies option funding → analytic/tree/simulation comparisons → funded hedge ledgers. A separate declared default state tests the proposed reserve. All these evidence streams reach the decision together. Altering a quote must propagate through the relevant calculations; changing only the displayed final price is not an intervention.

The stipulated demonstration gates

Require all five: (1) requested put rights remain European; (2) the proposed reserve funds every declared payment date without an injection, with cash-matched riskless zeros satisfying this by construction; (3) the quote covers max(fixed-riskless PV, actual selected reserve purchase PV) plus analytic put plus the additional initial cash calculated from the −1 CU tested-loss limit; (4) controlled estimated MC SE is at most 0.15 CU; and (5) the finest European tree differs from the analytic European put by at most 0.03 CU. The maximum preserves the conservative riskless-liability floor and also finances a selected reserve that costs more. Equality passes; the floating quote comparison allows 10−10 CU numerical tolerance. These are author-designed classroom rules. They are neither an optimized commercial risk policy nor regulatory approval. A failure cannot be averaged away by four other passes.

Passing means eligible for a limited classroom demonstration. It does not validate the return process, prove a loss bound outside supplied paths, establish a borrowable credit line, clear a changed exercise contract or authorize a real trade. The root continuation hedge is not an instruction to continue after an optimal immediate exercise. The same distinction between a computation and its permitted use must appear in your own interface.

Complete instructor guide, numerical benchmarks and transfer answers

1. Contract map and exact curve

The fixed and put cash add because present valuation is linear. Their statistical independence is unnecessary. Default of the reserve issuer changes an asset cash stream, while the liability schedule remains 3, 3, 103 plus the separately owed put. The bank/stock positions hedge the put liability; positive put delta is not the correct sign here. The price received at time zero must be allocated among the dated reserve, option hedge and any added cash budget before profit is discussed.

Triangular calibration gives D1 = 98/100 = 49/50, D2 = (100.79 − 3D1)/103 = 19/20, and D3 = (96.68 − 2D1 − 2D2)/102 = 91/100. Substitution returns every quote exactly. Fixed PV is 3(.98) + 3(.95) + 103(.91) = 99.52, with pieces 2.94, 2.85 and 93.73. Holding zeros paying 3, 3, 103 matches each dated liability without future sales. This is stronger than matching one yield or one duration.

The simple interval forward rates are 2/49, 6/95 and 8/91 per year. For example, .98/.95 is the bank gross growth over years 0.5–1, so its simple annualized rate is (.98/.95 − 1)/.5 = 6/95. Continuous node rates are approximately .040405415, .051293294 and .062873786. These describe the selected deterministic curve. They do not separate expected physical rate movements from a term premium or identify a stochastic-rate process.

3. Constant-rate reference and finite stopping

Over the put's life, r = −2 log(.98). Under the stated Q law, St = 100 exp[(r − σ²/2)t + σWt]. Applying the proved scalar Itô formula to the exponential gives dS = rSdt + σSdW. The normalized exponential has mean one by the Gaussian moment-generating function, so E[e−rtSt] = 100. Its finite second moment makes the discounted stock integral a true martingale on the finite horizon. This step requires the Gaussian model and bounded constant coefficient, not just notation calling a process a local martingale.

For a C1,2 interior option value, hold VS shares and bank cash V − SVS; self-financing gains and Itô give Vt + rSVS + ½σ²S²VSS − rV = 0. The put has terminal payoff (K − S)+, boundary V(t,0) = Ke−r(T−t) and vanishing value as S grows. The continuous at-most-linear-growth solution and stopped/martingale justification are those proved in Chapter 8; this project does not infer uniqueness from a numerical grid. The primary Black–Scholes notes supply context, not empirical validation.

4. Numerical and sampling evidence

The lognormal terminal payoff integral is an independent anchor. Put its kink at z* = [log(K/S0) − (r − σ²/2)T]/(σ√T), integrate the smooth positive-payoff part, and retain the discarded normal tails in the error register. The toolkit's 256/512-interval Simpson values are 8.688302327905 and 8.688302053310. Their difference is −2.74594 × 10−7; the fine value differs from the analytic formula by 1.82927 × 10−8. Because the put is bounded by KD = 102.9 after discounting, both omitted tails are at most 2KDΦ(−10) ≈ 1.56817 × 10−21. That is a genuine ideal-law tail bound. The Simpson refinement difference is a diagnostic, not a rigorously certified integration error. Independent adaptive integration gives approximately 8.688302035019.

Y = e−rT(K − ST)+, X = e−rTST, E[X] = S0.
Yβ = Y − β(X − S0), E[Yβ] = E[Y], Var(Yβ) = Var(Y) + β²Var(X) − 2βCov(Y,X).
A(Z) = [Y(Z) + Y(−Z)]/2, E[A] = E[Y], Var(A) = [Var(Y) + Cov(Y(Z),Y(−Z))]/2.

Square-integrability follows from finite lognormal moments. The fixed β = −.5 gives Y + .5(X − 100), not Y − .5(X − 100). An increasing control and decreasing put have negative covariance; for the particular ideal baseline, independent payoff integration yields Var(Y) ≈ 106.463456912, Cov(Y,X) ≈ −156.753973262, controlled variance ≈ 29.068002398, and antithetic-pair variance ≈ 16.797720226. This supports the chosen coefficient in this case, not a universal improvement for arbitrary β. If β were estimated, an independent pilot or a separately justified estimation scheme would be required. The reference uses no fitted coefficient.

Plain and controlled observations use both ideal independent Box–Muller normals per base draw. Antithetics use the cosine normal and its negative as one pair; counting 4,096 independent antithetic units would understate SE. The three estimators share inputs and are not independent experiments. At equal payoff count, the ideal pair-to-plain mean-variance ratio is 2Var(A)/Var(Y), not Var(A)/Var(Y). Sample comparisons should also include computing cost before claiming general efficiency.

At fixed n ideal independent units, s²/(n) estimates mean variance and the CLT motivates mean ± 1.96s/√n asymptotically. The controlled payoff is unbounded, so that same bound cannot be copied to it. The SHA256/open-midpoint generator produces a reproducible finite pseudo experiment, not a proof of iid continuous-normal coverage. A seeded interval may miss the truth; changing seeds until it contains the analytic price invalidates the stated experiment. These distinctions follow the scoped output-analysis and variance-reduction sources.

7. Decision, evidence and limitations

The baseline passes the European-rights gate, controlled SE gate (.085861373 ≤ .15) and finest European tree comparison (|8.706023840303 − 8.688302035018| ≈ .017721805 ≤ .03). It fails dated reserve funding and the quote's tested-loss budget: 108.5 < 118.944870090400. The correct output is revise_before_demo, with both failures visible. The 6.018 reserve discount must not be subtracted from the cash reserve needed to honor the client obligation.

The reserve purchase cost must also be covered when it exceeds the riskless floor. Set all three coupons to zero and specify default and full-face recovery at the first date, .5. The riskless promise costs 100(.91) = 91, but the selected risky reserve costs .9(91) + .1[100(.98)] = 91.7. Its signed apparent_initial_saving is −.7. The early 100 funds every later fixed payment in default, leaving 100/13 at year 1.5, but that future, default-only excess cannot pay for the initial reserve purchase or fund the hedge in survival. The required quote is therefore 91.7 + 8.688302035018 + 10.736568055382 = 111.124870090400. Quote 110.5 fails funding alone; at the allowed eight decimal places, 111.12487009 still fails and 111.12487010 passes the five classroom gates. Selecting matched zeros instead costs 91 and lowers the threshold to 110.424870090400, so 110.5 passes for that selection. The output records both selected_reserve_pv and reserve_budget_pv; the separately labeled frictionless model quote continues to use the riskless fixed-liability reference.

A defensible memo declines the current package. It offers a limited demonstration only with matched zeros, adequate separately allocated initial hedge cash, unchanged European rights and the stated computational evidence. It requests execution/funding terms and a broader, justified path model before any larger use. An alternative is to simplify the promise or refuse the proposed commercial quote. Higher client willingness to pay, a guaranteed hedge and a real financing facility are not created by a passing JSON flag.

For independent validation, solve the calibration as a simultaneous linear system; price European trees by terminal binomial sums; enumerate the four-step stopping policies; integrate the Gaussian payoff with a method different from the implementation; reconstruct discounted hedge gains rather than repeating the bank loop; and compute reserve funding from cumulative discounted cash deficits. These checks isolate implementation mistakes. They do not identify the correct future law or contract, establish empirical coverage or replace human judgment. The reference checker also exercises negative rates, altered quotes, recovery times, stock histories, costs, control signs and exercise requests, and rejects malformed or out-of-domain inputs under normal and optimized execution.
