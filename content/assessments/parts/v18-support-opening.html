Volume 18 assessment — strategy, governance and institutional leadership

Defend a feasible institutional decision, preserve the evidence behind it and distinguish a reasoned proposal from demonstrated responsibility.

This package contains a prerequisite diagnostic, two distinct written forms, an academic-and-financial leadership rehearsal, oral transfer and delayed retention. Every supplied institution, event, candidate, outcome and feedback record is an original synthetic specimen. The exercises can demonstrate the learner's reasoning on those specimens. Actual leadership requires authorized responsibility, contemporaneous action and resource records, observed consequences, external feedback and independent human evaluation. A correct answer, an approved plan and a completed action are different kinds of evidence.

  3. Prove the whole choice. Choose nonnegative divisible quantities x,y with x+y≤8 and x+2y≤12. Maximize P=3x+4y. Derive a global upper bound as a positive combination of the constraints, find an attaining allocation and establish whether it is unique.

  8. Evaluate the supplied evidence. Administration, scoring and real responsibility

Forms A and B each have twenty five-point prompts across five20-point domains: strategy/resources; people/organization; governance/academic leadership; finance/operations; and crisis/evidence/integration. Proposed duration is240 minutes, closed book with a basic calculator and supplied materials, without code or external search. Require85/100 overall,15/20 in every domain, correction of critical errors and an unseen transfer response. Form B is a distinct reassessment; equivalence of difficulty has not been psychometrically established.

Grade the requested reasoning and actual written artifact. Equivalent valid choices earn the stated criterion points when they use consistent assumptions, feasible resources and the specified decision rule. Preserve carried-through arithmetic errors separately from a false model, changed denominator, unauthorized action or fabricated evidence. A passing aggregate score cannot excuse a material cash shortfall, unsupported legal/causal claim, concealed conflict or invented completed responsibility. Record the actual first response, feedback and correction.

The project is open resource and requires actual execution of the supplied standard-library analysis plus complete institutional decision artifacts. Its supplied events and feedback are fictional rehearsal. The separate cross-volume leadership portfolio requires the learner's own real responsibility, strategy, budget, governance, people plan, crisis exercise and retrospective, with external human assessment. No exam score establishes an appointment qualification or universal readiness to lead an institution.
